# Rokid Relay v0.1.15-preview.13

Release type: preview hotfix
Date: 2026-07-02

## Highlights

- Adds self-arm recovery for the Rokid RG 1.21.009 Accessibility force-stop/removal bug.
- Provisions a versioned shell watchdog from the phone companion when Relay is armed.
- Lets the glasses helper re-run recovery on direct app launch and `BOOT_COMPLETED` when `armed=true`.
- Supports direct secure-settings repair if `WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS` is granted, and ADB loopback repair only with a provisioned key that was already authorized by the glasses.
- Adds a `Self-arm recovery` setup row and diagnostics status.

## Test Focus

- Start Relay from the phone app and confirm the glasses helper updates to `0.1.10-preview.7`.
- Confirm the phone setup row shows `Self-arm provisioned` after the CXR-L bootstrap reaches the glasses app.
- With an authorized ADB key provisioned in the phone app files, confirm `/data/local/tmp/rokid-relay-a11y-watchdog.sh status` reports the watchdog running.
- Force-stop the glasses package, remove the Relay Accessibility service from `enabled_accessibility_services`, open the glasses app, and confirm Accessibility plus the watchdog recover.
- Tap Stop/Disable in the phone app and confirm opening the glasses app no longer re-arms recovery.
