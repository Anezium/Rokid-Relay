# Rokid Relay v0.1.15-preview.14

Release type: preview hotfix
Date: 2026-07-03

## Highlights

- Fixes a stale phone-side self-arm state where the setup row could show `Disable pending` even though the glasses recovery was not armed.
- Adds a migration marker for real disable requests so old test/preview prefs are cleaned without hiding genuine pending disables.
- Leaves the glasses helper unchanged from `0.1.10-preview.7`.

## Test Focus

- Upgrade from `v0.1.15-preview.13` with `self_arm_disable_pending=true`, `self_arm_provisioned=false`, then open the phone app and confirm the `Disable pending` row clears.
- Start Relay normally and confirm self-arm still provisions only after the glasses ACKs recovery startup.
- Tap Stop/Disable after a real provision and confirm a pending disable remains visible until the glasses ACKs `disabled=true`.
