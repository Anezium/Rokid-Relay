# Rokid Relay v0.1.15-preview.5

Release type: preview
Date: 2026-06-20

## Highlights

- Replaces the `v0.1.15-preview.4` pending-inbox keepalive with a BLE wake bridge.
- CXR-L no longer stays awake just because the phone still has replyable inbox entries.
- While Relay is armed, the phone advertises a small BLE GATT wake endpoint.
- If the glasses inbox is open while the phone link is sleeping, tapping reply sends a BLE wake request with the selected `notificationId`.
- The phone refreshes active notifications, starts CXR-L, and the glasses automatically send `start_voice` once the CXR link returns.
- The inbox remains visible locally on the glasses while the phone sleeps.

## Test Focus

- Install the phone APK and updated glasses helper, open Rokid Relay, grant Bluetooth permissions, then press `Start`.
- Send a replyable notification and confirm it appears on the glasses and remains in the inbox.
- Wait longer than the two-minute CXR-L idle window; the phone foreground notification should disappear.
- Open the old inbox entry on the glasses and tap reply.
- Confirm the glasses show a wake message, CXR-L reconnects, and voice capture starts without waiting for a new notification.
- After reply/cancel, confirm CXR-L sleeps again instead of staying awake because inbox entries still exist.
- Battery-test with Relay armed and a pending inbox entry; CXR-L should be off until notification or inbox reply wake.
