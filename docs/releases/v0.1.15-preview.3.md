# Rokid Relay v0.1.15-preview.3

Release type: preview
Date: 2026-06-20

## Highlights

- Fixes the inbox UX regression from `v0.1.15-preview.2`.
- The glasses helper now keeps the local inbox visible when the phone enters wake-on-notification sleep.
- The inbox shows the sleeping state, and reply start is blocked while the phone link is asleep.
- Relay still wakes on new replyable notifications, forwards them to the glasses, and sleeps after the reply window.

## Test Focus

- Install the phone APK and updated glasses helper, open Rokid Relay, press `Start`, then close the app.
- Send a replyable notification and confirm it appears on the glasses and remains in the inbox.
- Wait for the idle sleep window to expire, then open the glasses inbox. Existing entries should still be visible.
- Open an old inbox entry and try to reply. It should show that the phone is sleeping instead of entering voice mode.
- Send another replyable notification and confirm Relay wakes again.
- Repeat the battery test with Relay armed but idle.
