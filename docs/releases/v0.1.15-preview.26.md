# Rokid Relay v0.1.15-preview.26

Release type: preview feature
Date: 2026-07-08

## Highlights

- The phone now shows a definitive, glasses-confirmed recovery status instead of guessing from its own records. When the glasses report that self-arm recovery is fully in place, the Self-arm recovery row turns green and reads **"Armed on the glasses — they recover on their own"** — no more ambiguous orange while everything is actually fine.
- The glasses helper reports its real state over the link: armed flag, recovery key present, `WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS` grant, accessibility service state, and helper version. The report is sent when the link comes up and whenever the state changes.
- If the phone believes recovery is armed but the glasses report it is not, the row now says so honestly: **"Glasses report recovery disarmed — tap Re-arm"**.
- Diagnostics gains a `Self-arm (glasses): armed=… key=… grant=… a11y=… helper=vc…` line, so support screenshots show the glasses-side truth at a glance.

## Notes

- The green confirmation deliberately ignores the live accessibility state: the entire point of the grant + watchdog is that the glasses can restore accessibility on their own after a fold or reboot.
- A pending disable request still outranks the glasses-confirmed state, so a disarm in progress is never masked by a stale "armed" report.
- Full backward compatibility: an older phone ignores the new report; an older glasses helper simply never sends it and the row behaves exactly as before.

## Verification

- On real hardware: with the glasses armed (key present, grant held), starting the relay produced the glasses report within seconds; the row went green with the exact confirmed wording and the report persisted on the phone (`armed=true key=true grant=true a11y=true helper=vc22`).
- Precedence checked on-device: with a disable pending, the row kept showing "Disable pending" instead of the green confirmation until the disable resolved.
- Phone unit tests pass (row-state precedence covered: confirmed-green, disarmed-mismatch, absent-report unchanged, disable-pending priority); both APKs build.

## Test Focus

- With everything already set up, open the app and confirm the Self-arm recovery row is green with "Armed on the glasses — they recover on their own" once the link is up.
- Disarm recovery from the glasses side (or wipe the helper's data), reconnect, and confirm the row flips to "Glasses report recovery disarmed — tap Re-arm".
