# Rokid Relay v0.1.15-preview.1

Release type: preview
Date: 2026-06-20

## Highlights

- Relay now uses wake-on-notification behavior instead of holding CXR-L open in the background.
- Press `Start` once to arm Relay. Replyable notifications wake the phone service, forward to the glasses, then let the service sleep after the reply window.
- Passive system events such as boot, app update, Bluetooth reconnect, notification-listener connection, and normal app open no longer keep CXR running.
- Android CXR microphone foreground mode is requested only while voice capture is active.

## Test Focus

- Install the phone APK, open Rokid Relay, press `Start`, then close the app.
- Confirm the foreground notification disappears after the idle window when no reply is active.
- Send a replyable notification and confirm Relay wakes, forwards it to the glasses, and sleeps again after roughly two minutes.
- Repeat the battery test with Relay armed but idle.
