# Rokid Relay v0.1.15

Release type: stable
Date: 2026-07-08

This is the stable release of the 0.1.15 line, closing out twenty-seven preview builds. If you are on any `v0.1.15-preview.*` build, update to this one; everything below is included.

## Highlights since v0.1.14

### Battery: wake-on-notification

Relay no longer holds the CXR-L link open in the background. Press **Start** once to arm it; replyable notifications wake the phone service, forward to the glasses, and let the service sleep again after the reply window. A BLE wake bridge lets the glasses wake the phone on demand, so an idle armed Relay costs close to nothing.

### Voice replies that survive real phones

The Android CXR speech engine was reworked end to end:

- Recognition sessions now implement Android's segmented-session callbacks, fixing replies that ended in `Android STT returned no text` even though the microphone clearly picked up speech.
- The app walks the phone's speech **recognizers** (system default, Google online, on-device) and each language's **locale chain** (for Cantonese: `yue-Hant-HK` → `yue-HK` → `zh-HK` → Auto) until one actually works, instead of trusting the first service Android lists.
- Transient recognizer disconnects retry instead of surfacing `Speech recognition error 11`.

### Self-arm recovery: the glasses look after themselves

Rokid RG firmware 1.21.009 force-stops the Relay accessibility service. The 0.1.15 line grew a complete recovery system so this never needs a PC again:

- One **Bootstrap** tap on the phone arms recovery. The glasses pair Wireless Debugging **with themselves over `127.0.0.1`**, so the phone's network is irrelevant — VPNs, client isolation, hotspot NAT, or being on different networks can no longer block setup. The glasses just need any Wi-Fi network; it doesn't even need internet.
- The bootstrap updates the glasses helper first, navigates the glasses Settings in any system language, and reads the pairing code on its own.
- Once armed, the glasses re-enable the accessibility service themselves after folding, force-stops, and reboots — the phone isn't involved anymore.
- The phone shows a glasses-confirmed status: green **"Armed on the glasses — they recover on their own"** when the glasses report in live, an honest **"last confirmed 2h ago"** when the report is remembered, and explicit **"tap Re-arm"** prompts when the glasses report a missing key or settings grant.

## New in this build (since preview.27)

- Fixed a relaunch loop in the glasses self-arm repair: on an armed device, every launch of the glasses app could re-trigger itself through the repair path, burning battery and starving other glasses apps. The repair is now idempotent — it only writes settings that actually changed, relaunches at most once per 30 seconds and only when a repair really happened, and the shell watchdog got the same backoff.
- The bootstrap failure hint no longer tells you to put the phone and glasses on the same Wi-Fi; since preview.23 that requirement doesn't exist. It now points at the thing that matters: the glasses' own Wi-Fi.

## Verification

- Phone and glasses unit test suites pass (140 tests).
- Relaunch fix verified on hardware: one genuine repair produces exactly one relaunch, then `already armed; relaunch skipped` with no further activity starts.
- The self-arm bootstrap chain (helper force-update → on-glasses `127.0.0.1` self-pairing → self-grant → glasses-confirmed report) was verified end to end on hardware during the preview cycle, including on a Russian-locale device.

## Known limitations

- After a phone reboot, some OEMs (Xiaomi/MIUI in particular) block the app's automatic restart until you enable **Autostart** for Rokid Relay and lift battery restrictions. The glasses' own recovery is unaffected.
- Glasses armed on an earlier preview keep their previously deployed watchdog script until the next Bootstrap run refreshes it.
