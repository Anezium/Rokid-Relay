# Rokid Relay v0.1.15-preview.2

Release type: preview
Date: 2026-06-20

## Highlights

- Fixes the wake-on-notification inbox edge case from `v0.1.15-preview.1`.
- Before the phone service sleeps, it now sends a `phone_sleeping` event to the glasses.
- The glasses helper clears stale replyable inbox entries when the phone sleeps and refuses to enter voice reply mode while the phone link is asleep.
- Relay still wakes on new replyable notifications, forwards them to the glasses, and sleeps after the reply window.

## Test Focus

- Install the phone APK, open Rokid Relay, press `Start`, then close the app.
- Send a replyable notification and confirm it appears on the glasses.
- Wait for the idle sleep window to expire, then open the glasses inbox. It should show that the phone is sleeping instead of offering a stale reply.
- Send another replyable notification and confirm Relay wakes again.
- Repeat the battery test with Relay armed but idle.
